At PrintersVault, we are committed to making our website accessible and easy to use for as many people as possible, including individuals with disabilities.

We believe that customers should be able to access information about our products, navigate our website, and use our online shopping features without unnecessary barriers.

1. Our Commitment to Accessibility

PrintersVault

We consider accessibility as an ongoing process and work to identify and address potential barriers that may affect how customers interact with our website.

The U.S. Department of Justice recognizes that inaccessible website features can create barriers for people with disabilities and identifies practices such as alternative text, keyboard navigation, accessible forms, captions, appropriate color contrast, and usable headings as important accessibility considerations.

2. Accessibility Guidelines

We aim to improve the accessibility of our website by following recognized web accessibility practices, including principles reflected in the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG).

Our accessibility efforts may include consideration of:

  • Keyboard navigation
  • Screen-reader compatibility
  • Alternative text for meaningful images
  • Clear headings and page structure
  • Readable text and sufficient color contrast
  • Accessible forms and input fields
  • Descriptive links and buttons
  • Zoom and text-resizing functionality
  • Accessible navigation and interactive elements
  • Captions or text alternatives for applicable multimedia content

Accessibility is an ongoing process, and not every part of the website may be fully accessible at all times.

3. Assistive Technologies

Our goal is to give services to customers who use assistive technologies to access the web.

Depending on the device and software being used, customers may interact with our website using technologies such as:

  • Screen readers
  • Keyboard navigation
  • Voice-control software
  • Browser zoom and text-resizing tools
  • Other accessibility features built into computers, tablets, and mobile devices

The accessibility experience can vary depending on the browser, operating system, assistive technology, and other settings used by an individual customer.

4. Ongoing Improvements

Website accessibility is an ongoing effort. We may periodically review and improve our website to identify accessibility barriers and enhance the overall user experience.

We may use a combination of manual reviews, automated accessibility testing, user feedback, and other appropriate methods when evaluating accessibility.

Automated accessibility tools can help identify potential issues, but they do not necessarily identify every accessibility barrier. Manual review and user feedback can also be important parts of an effective accessibility process.

5. Third-Party Content

Some portions of our website may include content, applications, payment features, videos, product information, or other functionality provided by third parties.

While we seek to provide an accessible experience across our website, we may not have complete control over the accessibility of third-party content or services.

Where practical, we encourage third-party providers to believe in accessibility standards and practices.

6. Accessibility Feedback

If you experience difficulty accessing any part of PrintersVault.com, we encourage you to let us know.

Your feedback can help us identify areas that may need improvement.

When contacting us, please provide:

  • The webpage or feature you were trying to access
  • A brief description of the accessibility issue
  • The type of assistive technology or device you were using, if applicable
  • Any other information that may help us understand the issue

You can contact us at:

Email: hello@PrintersVault.com

7. Alternative Assistance

If you are having difficulty accessing information or completing an activity on our website, please contact us.

We will make reasonable efforts to assist you and help provide the information or assistance you need through an available communication method.

8. Changes to This Accessibility Statement

We may update this Accessibility Statement periodically as our website, accessibility practices, or applicable requirements change.

Any updates will be reflected by changing the Last Updated date at the top of this page.
