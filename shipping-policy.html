At PrinterSetupExperts, we aim to provide a clear and reliable shipping experience for every order. This Shipping & Delivery Policy explains how we process, ship, track, and deliver orders placed through printersetupexperts.com.

This policy is designed around the key shipping topics covered by the reference policy, while being tailored specifically to PrinterSetupExperts and its US operations.

1. Shipping Destinations

PrinterSetupExperts currently ships eligible orders within the United States.

Shipping availability may vary depending on the product, destination, carrier requirements, product restrictions, and other logistical considerations.

We may restrict shipment of certain products to specific locations where required by manufacturer, carrier, regulatory, or logistical requirements.

2. Order Processing

Orders are generally processed after successful payment authorization and any necessary order verification.

Order processing time may vary depending on:

  • Product availability
  • Order volume
  • Payment verification
  • Shipping destination
  • Weekends and holidays
  • Additional order verification requirements

Orders placed on weekends or recognized holidays may begin processing on the next applicable business day.

If additional information is required to verify an order, processing may take longer.

3. Processing Time

Most orders are processed within the timeframe indicated on the website or during checkout.

Processing times are separate from shipping transit times. An order must first be processed before it can be handed over to the applicable shipping carrier.

Unexpected delays may occur due to inventory availability, payment verification, high order volume, holidays, or circumstances outside our reasonable control.

4. Shipping Methods

Available shipping methods may vary depending on:

  • Delivery location
  • Product type
  • Order size and weight
  • Carrier availability
  • Shipping service selected

Available shipping options and applicable charges will generally be displayed during checkout before you complete your purchase.

Not every shipping method will be available for every order or destination.

5. Shipping Charges

Applicable shipping charges are calculated based on factors such as:

  • Delivery destination
  • Product size and weight
  • Order value
  • Selected shipping method
  • Carrier rates
  • Applicable promotions

Any applicable shipping charges will be displayed during checkout before payment is completed.

If a promotional offer provides free or discounted shipping, the applicable terms of that promotion will apply.

6. Estimated Delivery Times

Delivery times depend on the shipping method, destination, product availability, carrier, and other circumstances affecting transportation.

Any delivery timeframe displayed on our website, during checkout, or in an order communication is an estimated timeframe and not a guaranteed delivery date, unless expressly stated otherwise.

Carrier delays, weather, holidays, transportation disruptions, and other events outside our reasonable control may affect estimated delivery dates.

7. United States Delivery

Orders shipped within the United States are delivered using available shipping carriers based on the destination, product, order, and selected shipping service.

Customers are responsible for providing a complete and accurate shipping address, including apartment, suite, unit, or building information where applicable.

Providing incomplete or incorrect delivery information may result in delays, additional charges, or an undeliverable shipment.

8. Order Tracking

When tracking information is available, it will generally be provided after your order has shipped.

Tracking information may take some time to become active after a shipping label has been created or after the carrier receives the package.

Once tracking becomes active, customers can use the provided tracking information to monitor the shipment through the applicable carrier.

9. Multiple Shipments

An order containing multiple products may occasionally be shipped in separate packages.

This means:

  • Products may arrive on different dates.
  • More than one tracking number may be provided.
  • One item arriving before another does not necessarily mean the remaining item is missing.

Please review all available tracking information before reporting an item as missing.

10. Accurate Shipping Information

Customers are responsible for providing accurate and complete shipping information during checkout.

Please carefully review:

  • Recipient name
  • Street address
  • Apartment, suite, or unit number
  • City
  • State
  • ZIP code
  • Contact information

PrinterSetupExperts is not responsible for delivery problems caused by incorrect or incomplete information supplied by the customer, except where otherwise required by applicable law.

11. Address Changes

If you need to change your shipping address after placing an order, contact us as soon as possible at:

hello@printerexpertshub.com

We will make reasonable efforts to accommodate an address-change request before the order is shipped.

However, we cannot guarantee that an address can be changed once an order has entered processing or has been transferred to a shipping carrier.

Certain address changes may require additional verification for security purposes.

12. Delivery Delays

Although we work to provide accurate shipping estimates, delivery delays may occasionally occur because of circumstances including:

  • Severe weather
  • Carrier disruptions
  • High shipping volumes
  • Holiday periods
  • Transportation delays
  • Incorrect or incomplete addresses
  • Product availability
  • Natural disasters
  • Government restrictions
  • Other circumstances outside our reasonable control

If tracking indicates that a shipment is delayed, customers may also contact the applicable carrier for the latest transportation information.

13. Lost Packages

If tracking information indicates that a shipment has not been delivered within a reasonable period, please contact us at hello@printerexpertshub.com.

We may review the available order and tracking information and, where appropriate, coordinate with the applicable carrier.

Additional information may be requested before a resolution is determined.

14. Packages Marked as Delivered

If tracking shows that your package has been delivered but you cannot locate it, we recommend:

  • Checking around the delivery location.
  • Checking with household members or others at the delivery address.
  • Checking your building's reception area, mailroom, or other designated delivery area, where applicable.
  • Reviewing the carrier's tracking information.
  • Allowing a reasonable amount of time in case the package was marked delivered before the physical delivery was completed.
  • Contacting the applicable shipping carrier for additional delivery information.

If the package still cannot be located, contact us with your order number and tracking information so we can review the matter.

15. Damaged Shipments

If your order arrives with visible shipping or packaging damage, please contact us promptly.

Please retain:

  • The damaged product
  • Original product packaging
  • Shipping box
  • Shipping label
  • Packing materials

We may request photographs of the product and packaging to help us review the claim.

Eligible damaged-product claims will be handled in accordance with our Refund & Return Policy.

16. Incorrect or Missing Items

If you receive an incorrect product or believe an item is missing from your shipment, contact us at hello@printerexpertshub.com with your order number and details of the issue.

Before reporting an item as missing, please check whether your order was divided into multiple shipments and review all available tracking information.

We will review the order and shipping information and provide appropriate next steps.

17. Undeliverable Packages

A shipment may be returned to us if:

  • The shipping address is incorrect or incomplete.
  • The carrier cannot access the delivery location.
  • Delivery attempts are unsuccessful.
  • The recipient refuses the shipment.
  • The package is not collected when required.
  • The carrier determines that the shipment cannot be delivered.

If an order is returned to us as undeliverable, we will review the circumstances and contact you when appropriate.

Additional shipping charges may apply if reshipment is requested because of an incorrect address or another customer-related delivery issue.

18. Refused Deliveries

Customers should contact PrinterSetupExperts before refusing delivery whenever possible.

Refusing delivery does not automatically qualify an order for a full refund.

If a refused shipment is returned to us, the order will be reviewed under our Refund & Return Policy, and applicable shipping or return costs may apply where permitted by law.

19. Shipping Restrictions

Certain products or destinations may be subject to shipping restrictions because of:

  • Carrier requirements
  • Product characteristics
  • Manufacturer restrictions
  • Regulatory requirements
  • Destination restrictions
  • Other logistical considerations

If an order cannot be fulfilled because of a shipping restriction, we will provide an appropriate resolution, which may include cancellation and refund of the affected product or order.

20. Order Verification & Shipping Security

To help protect customers and prevent unauthorized transactions, certain orders may require additional verification before shipment.

We may temporarily hold an order while verifying information such as:

  • Billing information
  • Shipping information
  • Payment authorization
  • Contact information
  • Other transaction details reasonably necessary for order verification

Failure to complete required verification may result in an order being delayed or canceled.

21. Risk of Loss

Responsibility for shipments and risk of loss will be determined in accordance with applicable law and the circumstances of the transaction.

Nothing in this policy is intended to limit consumer rights that cannot legally be waived or restricted.

22. Returns After Delivery

Shipping and delivery are separate from our return process.

If you successfully receive an order but later want to return an eligible product, please review our Refund & Return Policy for information regarding:

  • Return eligibility
  • Return timeframes
  • Return shipping responsibilities
  • Non-returnable products
  • Refund processing
  • Damaged or defective products

Please contact us before sending a product back.

23. Changes to This Policy

PrinterSetupExperts may update this Shipping & Delivery Policy periodically to reflect changes to our shipping practices, carriers, service areas, business operations, or applicable requirements.

The Last Updated date at the top of this page indicates when this policy was most recently revised.
